Karl worked on the autonomous electric vehicle developed by Formula Trinity for Formula Student UK.

He led a path-planning team of five in 2023/24, using lidar and visual data to generate optimal racing lines. In 2022/23 he worked on the control team, guiding the vehicle to precise waypoints by fine-tuning output signals.

The team received the Tata Elxsi Autonomous Best Design Award at Formula Student UK in Silverstone.
