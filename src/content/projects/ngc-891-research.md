This thesis and capstone research project was completed at the Center for Astrophysics | Harvard–Smithsonian and received a First-Class Honours mark.

The work used Sub-Millimeter Array interferometer observations to investigate the distribution and properties of Giant Molecular Clouds in NGC 891. It produced an extensive catalogue of clouds and investigated how their properties varied with vertical separation from the galactic plane.
