This funded undergraduate research project was completed with the Irish Low Frequency Array in Birr.

The work included analysis of solar emission spectra and a qualitative investigation of the Irish electrical grid’s vulnerability to solar events. The findings were presented as a poster at the Laidlaw Conference at the London School of Economics.
